# Taymar Solutions

## Register

**brand** — this is a marketing site for an outbound sales agency. The site IS the product impression. There is no app behind it.

## What Taymar does

Commission-first B2B outbound sales agency founded by Ben Marchiony in 2024. Five reps in Austin, TX. The team finds buyers, qualifies them, books meetings, and gets paid per booked-and-qualified meeting that shows up. No retainer, no SOW, no head-count.

Works with B2B clients across the US and Canada. Six core verticals: SaaS/tech, professional services, financial/insurance, construction/trades, healthcare services, logistics/supply chain. Will not run outreach for consumer-facing offers, sub-$5k deal sizes, crypto, or regulated securities.

## Users

Mixed buyer profile — the site must speak to all three without picking one:

- **Founders / CEOs at $1–10M companies.** No in-house SDR team. Deciding whether to outsource outbound entirely. They want proof the math works.
- **Heads of Sales / VPs at $5–50M companies.** Have a sales team, lack pipeline volume. Want augmentation without hiring more SDRs.
- **Marketing / RevOps leads** evaluating outbound as a pipeline channel alongside inbound and paid.

Job-to-be-done for all three: convince me this isn't another agency that will invoice me whether or not the phone rings.

## Strategic principles

1. **The pitch is the page.** Commission-first is the whole product. Every section reinforces "we get paid when you do." No section exists that doesn't push that point or qualify it.
2. **Disqualify out loud.** Honest about who Taymar can't help. "Probably not" lists, "honest notes," walk-away language. This is a trust play — the willingness to say no is the proof of integrity.
3. **No agency theater.** No decks, no strategy hours, no AI-agent claims, no fabricated metrics. If a number is on the site, it has to be real or clearly a range.
4. **Austin, local, small.** Five reps is a feature, not an apology. The site should feel like a real small shop, not a stretched-thin solo or a faceless enterprise.

## Brand personality

Three words: **tough, plainspoken, honest.**

Tough — doesn't try to charm. Doesn't soften. Talks like a foreman, not a salesperson.
Plainspoken — short sentences, real numbers, working English. No "leverage," "synergy," "transform."
Honest — admits limits, names walk-away cases, surfaces the bad-fit math on the first page.

Reference: Basecamp / 37signals. Plain language, opinionated, blunt copy, illustration or type-as-image over polished stock photography, slightly old-school confident.

## Anti-references

Hard refusals:

- **No SaaS / startup aesthetic.** No gradient hero, no glassmorphism, no purple-to-pink, no "Book a Demo" template, no rounded-card grid.
- **No serif display fonts.** No Newsreader, Fraunces, Lora, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Serif, any italic-display moment. The editorial-typographic trap is the thing we are explicitly leaving.
- **No fake metrics or testimonials.** If a customer quote isn't real, it doesn't exist. Ranges and "typical" framing only for unverified numbers.
- **No dark mode / theme toggle.** Single light/kraft palette. Inverted dark sections are fine as art direction; a user-facing toggle is not.

Aesthetic lane to NOT land in: editorial-typographic (display serif + small mono labels + ruled separators + monochromatic restraint). The previous design lived here. The new design must read instantly as something else — trade signage, industrial, Basecamp-blunt.

## Imagery

No real photography available. Path is illustrations / type-as-image / signage motifs. Big condensed type used as graphical anchor, oversized numerals, hand-set lockups. Avoid stock photo of headsets, handshakes, generic office.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast for all body and primary-action text. Forms must be keyboard-reachable with visible focus. Animation must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Site is single-column-readable down to ~360px width.

## Out of scope for the site

- No customer portal, login, dashboard.
- No blog at launch (may come later).
- No pricing calculator. Commission structures are drafted in the discovery call.
- No live chat. Email + form submission only.
